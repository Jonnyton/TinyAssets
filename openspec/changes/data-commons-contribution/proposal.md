## Why

`build-forward-platform-capabilities` records the data commons as a cross-slice target, but it is an umbrella: its design decision D1 forbids implementing an independently deployable slice through it, so every slice must first become a narrower OpenSpec change. Its task 3.2 does not merely need a successor of its own — it names the thing that has to exist first: *"Forge cannot emit a manifest before the manifest contract exists."* Task 3.3 says the same for training (*"mint invokes the data-commons validation contract"*), task 4.4 for hardware, and task 5.1 transitively for pooled ownership. The slice dependency ledger states it plainly: manifest and license validation is *"the admission gate every downstream training or hardware claim invokes."* Of the unowned slices, this is the one that unblocks the most downstream work, and this change is that slice.

It could not have been authored before 2026-07-25. Task 3.1 carried a second blocker beyond D1 — the STATUS `host-decision` row for target-spec PLAN conflicts — *"since manifest storage and private-data placement are two of the four positions that row is waiting on."* Both positions landed that day (PR #1761), and they landed as different **kinds** of answer:

- **Manifest storage is decided.** PLAN.md now scopes canonical storage per domain: the knowledge bundle is canonical for the commons, Postgres is canonical for the platform's transactional domains (catalog, ledger, inbox, market), and neither is canonical for the other's domain. A manifest entry is commons knowledge, so its canonical form is settled and its indexes are rebuildable derived state. That blocker is cleared.
- **Private-data placement is decided to stay open.** Custody is a scoped **open research question** — per-situation and user-chosen among host machine, private universe brain, vault, and platform-held, with none ruled in or out. The obligation on a lane is exact: do not encode either answer as settled, name the custody mode your lane assumes, scope the lane to it, and record the assumption. That is compliance, not a blocker, and it is what makes the private→commons promotion path in this change custody-*agnostic* rather than custody-deciding.

The third input is the host's 2026-07-25 framing of what the commons actually is: **an OKF system anyone can write to**, for community collaboration on shared and similar goals. The umbrella's `data-commons` delta was written as a dataset marketplace, and read alone it invites a dataset registry with its own tool surface. Read against the commons reframe it is something simpler and more useful: a contribution is a commons entry, discovery is a commons read, and a manifest is typed frontmatter on that entry. This change specifies it that way.

Nothing here is shipped behavior. What exists on `main` is adjacent, not equivalent: `wiki-commons` owns the page substrate, the seed taxonomy, the draft-then-promote gate, and the discovery/coordination scope split; `evaluation-outcomes-and-attribution` owns the append-only contribution ledger and attribution edges; `build-brain-canonical-store` is the unbuilt owner of the canonical bundle write path. No contract says what a dataset contribution *is*, what admits it, or what a consumer must call before using it.

## What Changes

- Make a commons contribution an ordinary commons entry written through `write_page` and found through `read_page`, with contribution kind, related Goals, license id, provenance class, and integrity references as frontmatter keys — no new handle, no dataset tool, no platform-owned contribution registry, no second commons write path.
- Keep the commons anyone-writable: any authenticated principal contributes without invitation, curation seat, or platform approval.
- Define a dataset contribution as an immutable content-addressed manifest entry that moves references rather than bytes, whose canonical form is the commons bundle and whose entry/full-text/vector stores are rebuildable derived indexes — and whose market or catalog rows are canonical only for their own transactional domain.
- Own full-provenance **manifest** validation as a fail-closed server-side admission gate that downstream consumers invoke rather than reimplement. This lane owns no license registry, restriction policy, or license-enforcement boundary; where a declared identifier must be resolved or composed, it consumes the landed `paid-market-economy` lattice.
- Require contamination, PII/privacy, integrity, deduplication, and quality results versioned and bound to the exact `manifest_hash`, with dedup as ordinary node work under the caller's own authority rather than a hidden platform service, and every result — pass or fail — retained in provenance.
- Require exactly one provenance class per example with its transformation lineage, record every actual upstream source without deciding what legal terms follow, and make Dataset Forge a forkable commons workflow graph over existing graph primitives rather than a platform service.
- Record contribution and derivation provenance on the platform's **existing** append-only contribution ledger and attribution edges, consuming their landed guarantees and the generic N-parent contract owned by `node-discovery-and-remix` rather than redefining either. Create no second lineage store. Widening the attribution edge's endpoint kinds to admit the generic `commons-artifact` class is carried as a minimal MODIFIED delta, because the as-built substrate's endpoint kinds are a closed set that a commons artifact is not in.
- Make private→commons promotion an explicit user act that is never automatic and never assumes the platform holds the private source; every custody mode promotes identically, an unreachable source reports rather than degrades, and the commons entry asserts no custody claim over the original.
- Keep this half non-monetary: a manifest carries no pricing, contributor-share, or settlement field at all, this lane stages no dataset-rights market, and a value-moving action refuses and names the `paid-market-economy` transaction boundary instead of opening a second accounting path.
- Keep curation non-blocking and post-hoc: direct authenticated commons writes admit without a pre-publication review gate, while curation and moderation annotate the admitted entry through the existing collaboration model.
- Carry manifest immutability as a `wiki-commons` MODIFIED delta: a freeform write targeting an existing immutable content-addressed page is refused on the write path, while every other promoted-page write keeps its landed in-place overwrite behavior.

### Ownership boundaries

- **Consumes unchanged contracts:** `wiki-commons` (page substrate, seed taxonomy, custom-category acceptance, draft-then-promote, compare-and-swap patch, and discovery/coordination scope split, except for the explicit immutable-target MODIFIED delta below), `identity-auth-and-access-control` (authenticated principal, auth-scope gate, visibility axes), `graph-execution-substrate` (Forge graphs are ordinary compiled graphs), `constraint-evaluation` + `evaluation-runtime-and-scenarios` (contamination, privacy, and quality gate evaluation), and `shared-goals-and-convergence` (the Goal a contribution relates to and the held-out sets contamination is measured against).
- **Consumes and does not rebuild — the attribution moat:** the append-only contribution ledger and attribution-edge substrate in `evaluation-outcomes-and-attribution` own idempotency, per-edge clamping, cycle rejection, and generation depth; the generic artifact-derivation contract in `node-discovery-and-remix` owns atomic all-parent recording, aggregate credit, retry idempotency, and rationale. This change records contribution and derivation *onto* those contracts and defines no second provenance store, credit graph, lineage table, or weaker dataset-only derivation path. The contribution ledger already carries a generic `source_artifact_id` / `source_artifact_kind` pair, so contribution *events* for a manifest need no change; the attribution *edge* needs only an endpoint-domain widening, carried as a MODIFIED delta rather than assumed (see below).
- **Consumes an unbuilt owner:** the canonical commons bundle, its explicit commit protocol, and its redaction ordering belong to `build-brain-canonical-store`. This change states that a manifest entry is canonical in the bundle and derived in the index; it defines no bundle write path, commit protocol, or organization abstraction, and it must not be implemented ahead of that owner's durability contract.
- **Consumes a second unbuilt owner for outbound access:** manifest *movement* needs no outbound path — references move, bytes do not — but a Forge graph's grant-gated corpus fetch is an external read, and `boundary-layer` requires a source node to bind a declared, user-granted, revocable connection class. That edge is real and is named rather than elided; ingress, grants, caps, and credential blindness stay with `outbound-boundary-layer`, and this change defines none of them.
- **Consumes a prerequisite whose owner is currently UNRESOLVED:** selecting the commons explicitly as a `write_page` destination. The gap is verifiable on `main` from code, not only from an audit: `tinyassets/universe_server.py:771-793` defines the single `write_page`, and its routing resolves an authenticated caller's omitted `universe_id` to that caller's home universe and returns a `relay_to_universe` directive, so an authenticated principal has no freeform commons write at all — only `kind=` issue filings reach the shared commons. The host decided the *shape* on 2026-07-25 (commons stays anyone-writable behind an explicit commons scope). **No change's tasks or write-set currently claims implementing that selector**: `reconcile-universe-personification-relay` tasks 6.1/6.7 own the person-dossier anti-collision *restriction* on the commons path, which presumes the selector rather than delivering it. So ownership is recorded as unresolved and task 0.5 must establish it before §1 is built; this change defines no `scope` parameter and no alternate commons write path under any outcome. (`docs/audits/2026-07-22-write-page-commons-residual.md` is the detailed analysis but lives only on the unmerged branch `claude/write-page-commons-residual`, and is partly stale: it found two `write_page` definitions, while `main` now has exactly one — `directory_server.py`'s is gone. Cite the code, not the audit.)
- **Delegates money:** every value-moving consequence settles through the single authenticated double-entry transaction boundary owned by the `paid-market-economy` capability (whose transport `paid-market-track-e-wave-2-transport` is currently building). Normative text names the *capability*, not the change slug — change names expire on archive, capabilities do not.
- **Leaves with the umbrella:** dataset pricing modes (umbrella task 3.1's monetary half) and frozen contributor settlement/apportionment (umbrella task 3.1's settlement half), plus training instruments and minting (3.3) and the F3 prohibition (3.4).

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `data-commons`: commons contribution and discovery over the canonical page handles, immutable content-addressed manifest entries that move references, the fail-closed manifest-admission gate every downstream consumer invokes, versioned contamination/privacy/quality gating bound to the exact manifest hash, per-example provenance classes with complete source lineage, Forge as a forkable commons workflow, lineage recorded on the existing attribution ledgers under the generic remix contract, an explicit custody-agnostic promotion path, and a contribution half with no pricing surface.

### Modified Capabilities

- `evaluation-outcomes-and-attribution`: the attribution-edge requirement gains a generic **`commons-artifact` endpoint kind** alongside the existing branch and node kinds, so derivation between content-addressed commons artifacts records on the same substrate. This is not optional framing — the as-built edge substrate's endpoint kinds are a **closed enumerated set that a commons artifact is not in** (`tinyassets/attribution/schema.py:33-47` constrains `parent_kind`/`child_kind` to `('branch','node')`, and `tinyassets/api/market.py:898-975` requires branch ids and writes the kinds as literal `'branch'`), so such an edge is *rejected*, not merely unwritten. No irreducibility finding supports a dataset-specific substrate kind, so the modification admits the generic artifact class once and changes only the endpoint domain; every landed clamp, cycle, depth, idempotency, and append-only guarantee remains owned by this capability and is not redefined by `data-commons`.
- `wiki-commons`: the existing promoted-page overwrite requirement gains a content-class exception for immutable content-addressed entries. A freeform write to an existing immutable entry is refused with a mint-a-new-version instruction and leaves the page/index unchanged; ordinary promoted pages still overwrite in place. This is a write-target rule, not a pre-publication review gate or a second write path.

**`data-commons` SHALL NOT sync without both MODIFIED owner deltas.** A synced manifest-lineage guarantee beside an attribution substrate whose canonical text admits only branch and node endpoints, or a synced immutability guarantee beside a wiki write contract that still overwrites the target, would be exactly the drift `reclassify-forward-vision-specs` removed.

Everything else composes over as-built contracts without contradicting one, and was checked rather than assumed: custom categories and non-seed frontmatter keys are already accepted by `wiki-commons`' seed-taxonomy requirement; the shared root commons is already ungated by the per-universe ownership ACL; contribution entries classify as discovery-audience under the existing default-scope rule with no migration; and the *contribution ledger* already carries a generic `source_artifact_id` / `source_artifact_kind` pair with no closed-set constraint, so contribution events for a manifest need no modification. If task 0.1 or 0.2 finds any of those as-built statements has changed, that contradiction is carried as a further MODIFIED delta before implementation rather than asserted only in the `data-commons` delta.

### Released from the umbrella

**Ownership is one active owner per released *requirement*.** As originally written the umbrella's convention read "each released delta has exactly one active successor owner", which was accurate while every release was whole-delta (tasks 1.1, 1.2, 2.1). This release, like the one for task 4.1, takes only part of a delta, so the convention is restated at the granularity that actually carries the invariant: **no requirement may have two active owners.** A delta may be split across owners provided the split is disjoint, both sides are named where a reader will look, and nothing is copied.

Four of the umbrella's six `data-commons` requirements were **physically moved** into this change and restated in its terms — *Dataset assets are content-addressed reference manifests*, the manifest-admission portion of *Data commons owns fail-closed manifest and license validation*, *Contamination, privacy, and quality gates precede gate-backed use*, and *Dataset Forge is a provenance-preserving commons workflow*. The moved license-policy text was removed because it reverses the host-pinned CC0/no-enforcement position; whether datasets are outside that position and whether enforcement is authorized are explicit host decisions in `design.md`. Two monetary requirements stay with the umbrella: *Dataset pricing is explicit and independent of compute pricing* and *Contributor settlement is frozen, exact, and auditable*. This lane carries none of their fields and stages no second paid surface.

**Concurrent amendment note.** The unmerged lane `claude/o5-demand-side` (PR #1771, open at authoring time) makes the *same* requirement-granularity amendment to the umbrella's ownership convention, for the same reason, from the same base. Whichever of the two lands second must reconcile the two edits into one amended sentence rather than stating it twice — task 8.6 carries that obligation.

## Impact

This is an active, unimplemented target change. Nothing in it is shipped behavior, nothing here may be synced into `openspec/specs/` while it is unbuilt, and `openspec archive` MUST NOT be run against it (archive syncs deltas as a side effect). On implementation it will affect the commons entry schema and its frontmatter keys, the manifest-admission path, the run and mint boundaries that must call that path before work begins, gate-result recording bound to manifest versions, the wiki write-target exception for immutable entries, the attribution-edge endpoint-kind constraint and its migration, the seeded Forge graphs shipped as commons content, and the promotion path from private material. It depends on the umbrella's cross-slice invariants D1–D8, on `build-brain-canonical-store` for the canonical bundle's durability contract, on `wiki-commons` for the page substrate and immutable-entry refusal, on `evaluation-outcomes-and-attribution` for the ledgers it records onto, on `node-discovery-and-remix` for generic N-parent derivation, on `boundary-layer` for any outbound corpus or storage access a Forge graph performs, and on a commons write-destination selector whose owner is currently unresolved. It does not build a license registry, license enforcement, pricing schema, contributor-share schema, or dataset-rights market. Landing it unblocks umbrella tasks 3.2 (Forge's manifest emission), 3.3 (training's manifest-validation call), and transitively 5.1 — none of which this change implements or unblocks by being *authored*. Any public surface it exposes additionally requires the §14 concurrency/load proof, live connector canaries, a rendered chatbot conversation, and freshness-stamped post-fix clean-use evidence before it is treated as landed.
