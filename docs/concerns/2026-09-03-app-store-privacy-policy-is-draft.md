# App Store privacy policy is still an explicitly unfinished legal draft

**Filed:** 2026-09-03
**Verified:** 2026-09-03, public `https://tinyassets.io/legal/` after PR #2798
(`76d795a1a3794fc3f3112121a063ca21b3175ce0`) deployed
**Severity:** P2

## Source (verbatim)

> “Draft v0 — pending securities-counsel confirmation. Defaults are best-effort,
> not legal advice.”

The earlier omission is resolved. The live page now introduces its privacy disclosure
with:

> “What the apps collect (web, iOS, Android, desktop).”

## Re-verification and scope

The App Store submission packet correctly points to
`https://tinyassets.io/legal#app-data`. The deployed public page contains the iOS
app-data disclosure, recipients, protection, retention/deletion, and contact routes.
The technical completeness issue is therefore resolved. The remaining issue is the
page's own legal status: it still labels itself **Draft v0** and says a real legal pass
is pending. Do not publish App Privacy answers or submit the app while representing
this URL as an approved policy. Before submission, the founder must approve the legal
copy or obtain the legal review the page itself says is pending; any resulting edit
must then land, deploy, and be re-verified at the public URL.

This finding does not authorize changing legal text, publishing privacy answers, or
submitting to App Review.
